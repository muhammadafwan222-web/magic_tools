✅ Struktur Prompt yang Efektif:

[Subject] + [Action/Scene] + [Style] + [Lighting] + [Quality] + [Details]

📝 Contoh:

"A majestic dragon flying over mountains at sunset, epic fantasy style, golden hour lighting, highly detailed, 8k, cinematic"

🎯 Keywords Berguna:

• Quality: highly detailed, 4k, 8k, masterpiece

• Lighting: golden hour, dramatic lighting, soft light

• Style: oil painting, watercolor, digital art, photorealistic

• Camera: wide angle, close-up, macro, aerial view
